Kristin Peterson KRISTIN PETERSON is a poet and filmmaker based in Milwaukee, WI. She has had her poetry published in the Great Lakes Review, Burdock '12, drupe fruits and by pity milk press, as well as with Rogue Agent and Flag + Void. She continues to write film scripts, poetry chapbooks and children's stories. Her delight in science, humor and media informs her poetry.
